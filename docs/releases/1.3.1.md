## Fixed

- **A stuck transcription no longer leaves Earshot waiting forever.** Transcribing and finding speakers
  now have a time limit: three times the recording's length, and at least 15 minutes. If a job runs past
  it, Earshot stops it, including the tools it started, and tells you it took too long.
- **The activity log never quotes your recordings.** When a tool failed, the log could hold the last lines
  of its error output, which can include words from the recording. The log now keeps only which step
  failed and its error code. The details are still shown in the app.
- **The activity log and your notes are private to your Mac account.** Other accounts on the same Mac can
  no longer read `Earshot.log` or `notes.json`.
- **Turning Dictation off now throws away what you were saying.** Before, switching it off in the middle
  of dictating still typed the words into the app in front.
- **A note can't start while a meeting recording is starting.** While macOS asked for microphone
  permission for a meeting, a note or a dictation could slip in and take the microphone.
- **Notes keeps 10,000 notes, and tells you if it ever removes old ones.** Before, notes past 2,000 were
  removed without a word.
- **Tidy up never holds a dictation for more than five seconds.** If Apple's on-device model took longer,
  Earshot waited for it anyway; now it types your words untidied at five seconds, as intended.

Everything else is unchanged.

## Install or update

With Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask earshot`.

Or download **Earshot-1.3.1.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Earshot** onto **Applications**. Choose
**Replace** if it asks. Your recordings, notes, models, settings and permissions stay as they are.

Check the version under **Earshot → About Earshot**. It should read `1.3.1`.

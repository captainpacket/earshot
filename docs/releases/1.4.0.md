## New

- **Type notes while you record a meeting.** The recording screen has a **Your notes** box under the
  timer. Type names, decisions and to-dos as you go. They are saved beside the recording, readable only
  by you, and its summary uses them. They are read by Apple's on-device model with the transcript, so
  nothing new leaves your Mac.
- **Failed recordings have buttons.** On Recordings, a recording that was never transcribed shows
  **Transcribe**, and one that failed shows **Try Again** and **Move to Trash**. Try Again repeats the
  step that failed: the transcription, finding speakers or the summary.
- **Search shows why something matched.** Results are grouped under Recordings, Notes and Settings, and
  the words you typed are highlighted.

## Changed

- **Settings looks like the Dictation page:** cards with a short explanation under each setting.
- **Translate is in Settings → Transcription,** and it now stays on after you quit. The switch in the
  window's title bar is gone.
- **The menu bar panel and the Listening pill are restyled** to match the main window. The pill shows
  sparkles while it tidies up what you said.
- **Small grey labels are easier to read.** Times, details, shortcut badges and the record bar's labels
  are bigger.
- **Notes asks before deleting a note,** since a deleted note can't be brought back.
- **The recording screen fits a narrow window.** The "no one has spoken" warning wraps instead of being
  cut off, and a long microphone name no longer pushes the screen wider than the window.

Your recordings, notes, models, settings and permissions stay as they are.

## Install or update

With Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask earshot`.

Or download **Earshot-1.4.0.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Earshot** onto **Applications**. Choose
**Replace** if it asks.

Check the version under **Earshot → About Earshot**. It should read `1.4.0`.

## Fixed

- **A summary no longer fails because of one passage.** Apple's on-device model has a safety filter,
  and ordinary meeting talk can trip it: swearing, medical or legal topics, talk about violence, or a
  word speech-to-text misheard. When it did, the whole summary failed with "Summary failed: Detected
  content likely to be unsafe". Now Earshot narrows the problem down to the passage the filter refused,
  leaves only that passage out, and summarizes the rest. A line under the overview says how many
  passages were left out. The transcript always keeps everything.
- If the filter refuses every passage of a recording, Earshot says so in plain words instead of showing
  Apple's message.

Everything still runs on your Mac with Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to a cloud model.

To get a summary for a recording that failed before, open it and click **Summarize**.

## Install or update

With Homebrew: `brew upgrade --cask earshot`.

Or download **Earshot-1.5.1.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Earshot** onto **Applications**. Choose
**Replace** if it asks.

Check the version under **Earshot → About Earshot**. It should read `1.5.1`.

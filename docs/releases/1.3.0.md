## Changed

- **Summaries keep the detail.** A long recording used to be boiled down twice, into at most 36 short
  points. An hour-long meeting came out as a handful of vague bullets, and some of them repeated five
  times. Now each part of the recording, about ten minutes of speech at a time, writes its own detailed
  sections, and Earshot merges them. A longer recording gets a longer summary. On a 56-minute meeting,
  the summary went from about 15 points to more than 100, and it now keeps the names, numbers,
  companies and prices that were said.
- **No more repeats.** The same point said twice, or reworded slightly, appears once, even across
  sections. Points that differ only in a number ("a 15-node cluster" and "a 5-node cluster") are kept
  apart.
- **Who said what.** Points and action items name the speaker ("Speaker 2 asked…") instead of "the
  speaker", and a long stretch of one person talking keeps their name on every part of it.
- **Only what was said.** A due date is kept only if someone actually said it, so there is no more
  "N/A" or invented "next quarter". Decisions and Open questions list only what was agreed or asked,
  and action items list only tasks someone committed to.
- **A title and overview for the whole recording**, not just its first few minutes.

A long recording can take a few minutes to summarize: an hour took between 1½ and 7 minutes in testing. Summaries you
already have stay as they are. To get a new one, open the recording and click **Summarize**. Earshot
asks first if you edited that summary.

Everything still runs on your Mac with Apple's on-device model.

## Install or update

Download **Earshot-1.3.0.dmg** below, open it, and drag **Earshot** onto **Applications**.
Choose **Replace** if it asks. Your recordings, summaries, models and settings stay as they are.
The app isn't notarized, so macOS may ask again: go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**
and click **Open Anyway**. After an update, macOS may also forget the Accessibility permission:
if Dictation stops typing, switch Earshot off and on in **Privacy & Security → Accessibility**.

Check the version under **Earshot → About Earshot**. It should read `1.3.0`.
